Protocol and audit

Every round used a fresh agent with the same baseline rules and no strategy hint. Agents submitted one word at a time and read its rank before choosing another. Unknown words and invalid formats consumed attempts. There were no human interventions.

All 20 Neon transcripts were reviewed. Their 830 commands are individual guesses in the assigned round. No project, dictionary or external-source access was found. All recorded Neon ranks were checked against the frozen tables.

Neon was configured as gemini-4-neon with thought_level low or medium. Antigravity did not report executedModel, so this field remains null. The audit verifies the configured model and effort.

Russian rounds were not run and do not enter these metrics. The earlier Sol trial contains 790 audited submissions. All four records have matching dataset and protocol fingerprints.
